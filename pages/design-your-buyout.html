Private buyout — FAQ

How much does it cost to book the whole wave pool in Hainan?

A one-hour private buyout of the PerfectSwell® Surf Pool at Riyue Bay starts at ¥6,600 on the Intermediate wave setting (15% off the ¥7,760 list rate). Advanced and Pro settings are ¥8,470 per hour (list ¥9,960). The price covers the entire pool for up to 20 surfers — it is per session, not per person.

How many surfers can join a private buyout session?

Up to 20 surfers share the pool in a private session. The whole lineup is yours — no strangers, no drop-ins, every wave goes to someone in your group.

Can we change wave settings during a private session?

Each one-hour session runs at the wave setting you choose — from beginner rollers to performance walls, barrels and air sections. Book back-to-back hours to switch settings between sessions; the trip designer on this page prices it live.

Surf China Co. is a PerfectSwell® Destination Partner. We coordinate your private sessions directly with the surf park, confirming your session times and wave programme before you arrive.

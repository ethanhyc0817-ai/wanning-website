    How It Runs

    What two hours
    at the pool looks like.

    A session is not just the time you spend in the water. Here is how the operator builds the slot — and why you should get there early.

    Before · 45 min

    Arrive early

    Come at least 45 minutes before your session. Tickets, changing, wetsuit and board pick-up all happen before the clock starts — leave yourself the room and you will not miss the slot.

    01 · 30 min

    Warm-up on land

    Stretch, a briefing, and a run through what you are about to try on the wave that is running. Your coach sets the plan for the session here.

    02 · 60 min

    In the water

    Your hour at the pool, on that hour’s setting, with a short changeover between rotations. This is the block shown on the calendar.

    03 · 30 min

    Poolside

    Cool down, soak, watch the next rotation go, and talk through what worked. The best coaching conversations tend to happen here.

    Good to Know

    Before you build
    your trip around it.

    Schedules can move

    The surf pool is outdoors. The operator can adjust or cancel sessions — lightning, typhoon conditions or extreme heat will close the park. We confirm your exact sessions before you travel, and again on the day.

    Staying at the resort helps

    Guests staying at the surf pool resort get their own booking window on each session. If a particular wave matters to you, staying on site is the simplest way to get on it.

    Pro waves are private

    The barrel and air settings do not appear on the public calendar. They run in private sessions, where your group has the pool to itself and picks the setting.

    We book it for you

    You do not need to work the calendar alone. Tell us your dates and your level, and we will line up the sessions that suit you and hold them as part of your trip.

    Frequently Asked

    Questions about
    the calendar.

    Why is the surf pool closed until 24 September 2026?

    The pool is closed from 25 August to 23 September 2026 for a scheduled experience upgrade, and reopens on Thursday 24 September. Ocean surfing, golf, and everything else in Wanning carries on as normal in that window — a lot of guests use it as an ocean-surf trip instead.

    How long is a session?

    The slot on the calendar is 60 minutes: about 30 minutes of instruction on land, around 25 minutes surfing, and a short changeover. The full experience package around it runs 120 minutes — a 30-minute land warm-up, the hour at the pool, and 30 minutes of poolside downtime.

    Can I do more than one session a day?

    Yes, and most guests who are here to progress do exactly that — commonly a beginner or intermediate setting during the day and a harder one in the evening. Tell us how many sessions a day you want and we will build the days around it.

    What if the setting I want is not running on my dates?

    Book a private session. Your group takes the whole pool for the hour and chooses the setting — including the pro barrel and air waves that never appear on the public schedule.

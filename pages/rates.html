The Short Answer

How much does it cost to surf the wave pool?

A public 55-minute session at the PerfectSwell® wave pool in Riyue Bay, Hainan costs CNY 328–528 per person depending on wave level and date, with rooms at the surf pool resort from CNY 700 per night and full private pool buyouts from CNY 7,760 per hour.

Below is the full rate board — the same one we quote from. Everything is in CNY (Chinese yuan / RMB), and every session is a 55-minute block on a single wave setting matched to your level.

Frequently Asked

Rates, answered.

How much does a surf session cost at the wave pool?

Public 55-minute sessions start from CNY 328 per person for Beginner, CNY 388 for Intermediate and CNY 498 for Advanced, with peak-calendar dates a little higher. Pro-level barrel and air settings run only in private sessions.

How much does it cost to rent the whole wave pool?

A private buyout is CNY 7,760 per hour for the Intermediate setting and CNY 9,960 per hour for the Advanced and Pro settings — the whole pool for your group, however many of you surf. Special offers apply on selected dates and group sizes, so send us your dates for your rate.

Do prices change through the year?

Prices are in CNY (Chinese yuan / RMB) and move with the season — rates rise a little from 1 July to 31 August and over Chinese national holidays. Send us your dates and we will confirm the exact figure.

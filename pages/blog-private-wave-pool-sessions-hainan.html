Imagine having a private wave pool session with only your friends on a tropical island. No crowds, no drop-ins, no one yelling from the beach. Just you and your crew trading perfect waves back to back.

Nowadays, most surf spots are either flat, packed, freezing — or somehow all three. You drive hours for a maybe, share the peak with fifty strangers, and count yourself lucky to get three good waves. In Hainan, China, it's completely different. You can book a private wave pool session, bring your friends, and choose the exact waves you want to surf — at the PerfectSwell® surf pool at Riyue Bay, the world's only wave pool on a tropical island.

Your crew, your waves, nobody else

A private session flips the usual surf-trip math on its head. Instead of competing for waves, the pool is booked for your group only. Every wave that rolls through has someone's name on it, and it's someone you came with. No jostling for position, no drop-ins, no sitting shoulder to shoulder with a crowd — just wave after wave, on demand, shared between friends.

That changes how a session feels. People take turns, cheer each other into sets, film each other's waves, and try things they'd never attempt at a crowded break. The wave count you'd normally scrape together over a week of ocean surfing can happen in a single session — and every ride is watched by your own crew from the water's edge.

Around twenty wave settings — everyone gets something fun

The PerfectSwell pool runs around twenty wave settings: barrels, air sections, performance walls for turns, and soft, forgiving beginner waves. In a private session, your group decides what the machine serves — and you can change it as you go.

That's the detail that makes private sessions work for mixed groups. The shortboarder chasing clips gets a ramp on repeat. The friend who's never stood up gets a mellow roller with room to learn. Nobody compromises, because the pool doesn't have to pick one kind of surfer to serve. See The Surf Session package for how a pool session is structured.

Barrels, airs, turns, beginner waves. With around twenty wave settings, everyone in your crew gets something fun — in the same session.

Built for surf camps, family trips and group getaways

A pool you control, with no crowds and waves dialed to any level, is close to the perfect venue for a group trip. Surf camps get consistent, repeatable waves for coaching progression instead of gambling on the ocean. Family trips work because kids and beginners learn on gentle settings while the more experienced surfers in the family get their own waves in the same session. And for a group getaway — that dream session with your best friends you've talked about for years — this is the version where everyone actually scores.

Between sessions, resorts sit steps from the pool, so the whole trip happens in one place: surf, eat, rest, repeat.

And when the session ends, you're still on a tropical island

Here's the best part. After the session, you're not driving three hours back home from some inland tank. You're still on a tropical island. World-class golf — including the best links course in China at The Dunes, and Blackstone at Mission Hills, a former world top-100 course played by Tiger Woods and Rory McIlroy — is a short hop away. When the swell turns on, Riyue Bay's ocean break is minutes from the pool. Add beaches, warm weather and Hainan's island cooking and Xinglong coffee, and the "rest" of the trip is a trip in itself.

That's what makes Hainan special: perfect waves on demand, and a real island around them.

Getting your crew here is the easy part

Most visitors travel visa-free: Hainan offers 30 days of visa-free entry for passport holders from the U.S., Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Canada and many other countries. There are direct flights from Sydney and Melbourne to Haikou (HAK), plus routes via Hong Kong, Singapore, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur and Seoul into Haikou or Sanya (SYX). Good to Know

Private wave pool sessions — FAQ

What is a private wave pool session?

You book the PerfectSwell pool at Riyue Bay for your own group — friends, family or surf camp. No strangers in the lineup, no drop-ins, no waiting: just your crew trading perfect waves back to back, with the machine dialed to the settings you choose.

What wave settings can we choose?

The pool has around twenty wave settings — barrels, air sections, performance walls for turns, and mellow beginner waves. You can switch settings during the session, so a mixed-ability group all gets waves that suit them.

Is a private session good for beginners and families?

Yes. With no crowds and forgiving beginner settings on tap, a private session is one of the easiest possible environments to learn or progress in — which is why it works so well for family trips, surf camps and group getaways with mixed abilities.

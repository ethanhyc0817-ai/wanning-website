What to pack for a Hainan surf & golf trip

Two sports, one tropical island, one bag. What to bring, what to rent when you land, and what to leave at home — the complete Riyue Bay checklist.

Published August 31, 2026
Read Time 6 min
Boards & Clubs Rentable here — travel light
Water Warm, tropical, year-round
Home/Blog/What to Pack

Packing for one sport is easy. Packing for two — on a trip that swings from a PerfectSwell® wave pool session in the morning to a links tee box in the afternoon — sounds like a luggage problem. It isn't. Hainan is a tropical island where the heavy, awkward gear (boards, clubs, wetsuits) can all be rented or arranged when you arrive, which means the smartest packing list for a Riyue Bay trip is shorter than you think. Here's exactly what to bring, what to skip, and what to sort out on the ground.

The golden rule: pack for the tropics, travel light

Everything about this trip argues for a small bag. The island is warm, the dress code is casual outside the golf course, and the two bulkiest items in any surf-and-golf kit — a board bag and a travel case of clubs — are both optional here. Quick-dry clothing, sun protection and the few personal items that have to fit you (shoes, glove, prescription anything) are the core. If you forget something, Hainan is also home to the biggest duty-free mall in the world, so a missing pair of sunglasses is an excuse to go shopping, not a crisis.

Surf gear: bring little, rent the rest

Boards

Unless you're deeply attached to your own quiver, skip the board bag. There's a full rental quiver at Riyue Bay — soft-tops and longboards through performance shortboards — matched to the pool's roughly 20 wave settings and to whatever the ocean is doing. If you do fly with a board, airlines into Haikou (HAK) and Sanya (SYX) carry board bags; our getting here guide covers the routes.

In the water

The water is warm year-round on a tropical island, so the standard kit is boardshorts or a swimsuit plus a rash guard — the rash guard is really sun armour. Wetsuits are available at session pick-up alongside boards, so even that stays out of your bag. Worth packing because they're personal: a leash you trust if you're particular, surf earplugs, zinc for your face, and a change of swimwear so something dry is always in rotation.

Golf gear: shoes and a glove, not a travel case

When we book your rounds at China's best courses — the links at Shenzhou Peninsula, the black-lava fairways at Mission Hills — we handle tee times, clubs, transport and English-speaking caddies. That makes club hire part of the arrangement, not a chore, and it's why most guests leave the travel case at home. What you should bring is the gear that has to fit: your golf shoes, your glove, and a couple of collared shirts for the course. A small stash of your preferred balls and a marker cost nothing in luggage space. Questions about a specific course? Ask through the golf inquiry page.

Sun, heat and the occasional downpour

The tropics are the one part of this list to take seriously. Between a morning in the pool, an afternoon walking eighteen, and golden hour back at the bay, you can log a lot of sun in a day. Pack high-SPF sunscreen (and plenty of it), a zinc stick, a brimmed hat that survives wind, polarised sunglasses, and a lightweight long-sleeve layer for walking between all of it. In the warmer months — typhoon season, which is also when the ocean can light up — a packable rain shell earns its place; our season-by-season guide explains what each part of the year looks like.

The bulkiest things in a surf-and-golf kit — boards, clubs, wetsuits — are all waiting for you at Riyue Bay. Pack the things that have to fit you, and let the island supply the rest.

Documents, money and the boring essentials

Hainan offers 30-day visa-free entry for passport holders from the U.S., Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Canada and many other countries — no application, just your passport. The full details are in our visa-free travel guide. Beyond the passport: your onward ticket details, travel insurance paperwork, and any prescription medication in its original packaging. Mobile payment is everywhere in China, so set up an international-card-friendly payment app before you fly and carry a little cash as backup.

Tech and extras

China runs on 220V — bring a universal travel adapter and you're covered. A power bank matters more here than on most trips, because your phone doubles as translator, payment method and camera. Add a dry bag for pool decks and beach days, and reef-safe sunscreen if you're paddling out in the ocean. That's the whole tech list; the point of this trip is that your hands are on a rail or a grip, not a screen.

What to leave at home

The board bag, unless your board is non-negotiable. The club travel case — hire is part of how we book your rounds. Heavy wetsuits, formal clothes, and the “just in case” third pair of shoes. Your accommodation by the bay is minutes from the pool, so the daily loop is short and the laundry logic is simple: fewer clothes, washed more often, all of them quick-dry.

Good to Know

Packing for Hainan — FAQ

Do I need to bring my own surfboard to Hainan?

No. There's a full rental quiver at Riyue Bay — from soft-tops and longboards to performance shortboards — so most guests skip the board bag entirely. If you're attached to your own board, airlines flying into Haikou (HAK) and Sanya (SYX) carry board bags; otherwise rent here and travel light.

Do I need a wetsuit to surf at Riyue Bay?

For most of the year, no — Hainan is a tropical island and the water stays warm, so boardshorts or a swimsuit with a rash guard for sun protection is the standard kit. Wetsuits are available at session pick-up alongside boards, so if you want one you don't need to pack it.

Should I bring my golf clubs on a Hainan surf and golf trip?

Only if you want to. When we book your rounds we handle tee times, clubs, transport and English-speaking caddies, so club hire is part of the arrangement. Bring your own glove and golf shoes for fit and comfort, pack a collared shirt, and leave the travel case at home.

What documents do I need to travel to Hainan?

Passport holders from the U.S., Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Canada and many other countries get 30-day visa-free entry to Hainan — no visa application needed. Bring your passport, your onward ticket details and travel insurance documents, and you're set.

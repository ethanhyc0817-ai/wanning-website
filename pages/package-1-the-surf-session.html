FAQ

The questions everyone asks.

How many surf sessions do I get?

The package includes 2 sessions per day — 6 in total on 3 nights, 8 on 4 nights. As a couple, you share them: one session each per day. Travelling solo, all of them are yours. Want more water time? Extra sessions are US$80 each.

Do I need surf experience?

No. PerfectSwell® dials from soft, beginner-friendly waves up to steeper open faces. Sessions are matched to your level — first-timers and experienced surfers both fit.

Can I add more sessions?

Yes — US$80 per extra session, on request. The package includes 2 sessions per day (one each for a couple) as standard.

Do I need to bring a board?

Beginner softboards are available for the included surf-pool session setup. Performance or premium boards, and boards used outside that setup, can be rented separately.

Lefts or rights?

The pool alternates daily — lefts on odd calendar days, rights on even. Once dates lock, we’ll tell you exactly which days are which.

Where should I fly into?

Haikou Meilan (HAK) is the usual entry point; Sanya Phoenix (SYX) also works. Round-trip Premium Car transfer to Riyue Bay Surf Resort in Wanning is included from either.

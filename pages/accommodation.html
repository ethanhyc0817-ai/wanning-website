The Layout

The hotel and the surf pool are one place.

Everything in this photo is the same property — UECHAO Hotel Riyuebay. The rooms are the whole block sitting behind the beach — you walk out of the lounge and they’re right in front of you, about a minute away.

  • 1Standard roomsThe long block along the back — Ocean & Pool View and Garden View rooms, on floors that look out over the pool and the sea beyond it.
  • 2Courtyard rooms & Owner’s SuiteThe low buildings between the hotel and the beach, wrapped in tropical gardens. The Owner’s Suite sits here too — closest to the pool deck.
  • 3Lounge, dining & surf clubOn the pool deck. Step out of it and you’re looking straight at your room.
  • 4PerfectSwell® surf poolThe lineup itself — about a minute from your door, boards and all.

No taxis, no shuttle, no gate between the two. You are staying inside the surf park — the rooms and the water are next to each other, so breakfast, your board, the lounge and the lineup are all about a minute from your door. The sea is on the other side of the same grounds.

The Grounds

Garden, pool, and the sea.

The resort opens onto a palm-lined garden and a quiet lap pool — with the surf pool and the beach just a short path away.

Breakfast, Included

Resort Amenities

Everything a surf trip needs.

Beyond the rooms, the resort is fitted out like a proper surf basecamp — train, rinse, recover, repeat.

Fitness Center

A fully equipped gym looking onto the gardens — free for all guests, ideal for staying paddle-fit between sessions.

Free Guest Laundry

A self-service laundry room with washers and dryers, free of charge — a small thing that matters on a week of two-session days.
